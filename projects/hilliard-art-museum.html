Hilliard Art Museum

Print design and branding for the Fall 2026 season

BRAnding

graphics

While working at Between State, I've had many chances to design for Hilliard Art Museum in Lafayette, Louisiana, specifically for their Fall 2026 exhibitions. I've worked with them to create print pieces such as postcards, brochures, and mailers, as well as email newsletters, updating their website, and branding the four exhibitions opening in the fall.

Fall Events Mailer

Hilliard wanted an interesting and unexpected mailer to announce their fall exhibitions, and I was given the opportunity to figure out how it would be constructed and designed. I opted for a fold-out magazine style mailer to give the piece an editorial feel while still being condensed into the size of a regular envelope.

Curtis Patterson

Curtis Patterson is a Louisiana native building sculptures out of bronze or steel and focusing on forms that evoke jazz, motifs of the African diaspora, and motion.

A Century in Focus

A Century in Focus: Images from Women Photographers of the 20th Century features 20 female photographers and their works that shaped the field of photography.

Elemore Morgan Jr.

Elemore Morgan Jr. photographed the Smithsonian's Festival of American Folklife in 1976, and only now are the photos being shown in exhibition. The photos feature a variety of cultures and musicians, giving an inside glimpse of the festival.

Exhibition Branding:

Matthew Day Jackson

Matthew Day Jackson's work focuses on the materials and items that shape domestic life, including the dangerous materials used in the 20th century such as lead. Through his sculptures, paintings, and furniture designs, he connects the past with the present and turns the museum into a domestic space.
