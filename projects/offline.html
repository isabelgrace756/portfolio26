Offline

Reducing social media addiction by reframing habit change as a social experience

animation

BRAnding

graphics

ux

Offline is an app driven by transparency and the threat of bad habits being exposed to your friends. Team up with your friends to compete for screentime stats, pair with a buddy to keep each other accountable, and bring your screentime habits to light. Offline features a live activity widget which lets you nudge your friends if you catch them on a restricted app, sending a fullscreen popup to their phone letting them know you are aware of their behavior.

Make being Offline
cool again.

The Challenge

It's no secret that Gen-Z has a problem with screentime habits. Users want a new and more powerful way to curb their screentime addictions. The challenge is to create an intervention that does not solely rely on self-discipline, but rather social pressure and engagement to build better habits.
